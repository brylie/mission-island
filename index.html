Crew guide

From Seabrook, follow the main road west to Harbor Depot. Look for “Your home” at the cottages on the north side. Press E at your tool chest to take or store tools, or at your wardrobe to change clothes. Your first visit to the chest includes a carrot seed.

At night, press E at your own door. Dawn starts when every connected crewmate turns in. Press E again or walk away to cancel.

Between shifts

The gravel turn east of Harbor Depot leads to the closed ferry terminal. The damaged ramp needs a crew to repair it someday.

Take a seed to Island Farm off the beach trail south of Seabrook. Equip the hoe to till a plot, then press E to plant.

Look for the orange rack on the north end of the minimap. Windstone Ridge lends a free glider. A successful checkout opens the Mirror Pool survey for both players.

Follow the depot trail past the freight yard to the small wooden fishing dock. Mara works there after dawn and heads home at dusk.

Field journal

    The journal notes a fish when you land it and a place when you walk there. Aim at an animal, a plant or a landmark and press E to record it. Both of you share one journal.
