# Living world survey

This pass covers [starter destinations](https://github.com/brylie/mission-island/issues/25), [Saltwind destinations](https://github.com/brylie/mission-island/issues/26), [biomes](https://github.com/brylie/mission-island/issues/27), [flora](https://github.com/brylie/mission-island/issues/28), [wildlife](https://github.com/brylie/mission-island/issues/29), [discovery walks](https://github.com/brylie/mission-island/issues/30), and [local phenomena](https://github.com/brylie/mission-island/issues/31).

Coordinates are metres, written (X, Z). Set them in the starter Landscape Workbench. On Saltwind, choose **Site map → Survey location → Inspect site** to begin at the road approach. The map also has clear daylight, dusk, morning, rain, and night presets. Set **Overview** for the whole-island silhouette and switch back for pedestrian-scale details. The Saltwind approach and pad checks extend the [original geometry survey](saltwind-qa.md).

## Habitat route and observation conditions

The western loop runs from **Brinewick Coast** into **Reedwater Valley**, then climbs toward **Alder Crown**. Look for pale sand and low shore grasses, greener riverbanks with reeds and alder, then dark woodland with firs and ferns. **Barley Meadows** uses open warm greens and scattered flowers. **East Bluff** has grey stone tones and low heather. Terrain and map share the same continuous habitat weights. Saltwind's smaller plants use seeded scale, breadth and rotation variations, and drop out of the overview while treelines remain visible.

| Sighting or effect | Where and when to look |
| --- | --- |
| Schooling fish | Starter shallows west of the bay and off Driftwood, or the shallows off Sandspit Pier. Enter the water or take the starter skiff near a school to see it spread and veer away. Back off and wait for the group to collect. |
| Day flocks | Watch the starter harbor and Saltwind's coast or Alder Crown. In clear daylight the flock lands together in the latter part of its 48-second circuit. After dusk the starter flock and the Alder Crown birds settle at a tree. Heavy rain brings birds toward shelter. |
| Night bats | Look near Pinewoods and above the shore of Mirror Lake at night. A clear night around 23:00 gives the strongest activity. Heavy rain keeps them sheltered. |
| Dawn mist | Set 06:48, or 6.8 on the Workbench slider. Look into Mirror Pool or Mirror Lake. Mist dissipates by mid-morning and is thinner in heavy rain. |
| Waterfall spray | Check Fernfall or Tarnstep Falls on a wet afternoon and again in clear weather. Spray rises and spreads from a fixed stretch of the cascade. |
| Plankton | Visit Shellglass or Sandspit near 23:00 in calm weather. Look down into the shallows for turquoise glints. |

The wildlife blends motion from deterministic phase and placement data. The starter host broadcasts the elapsed island time, rain, crew positions and skiff position. Both browsers use those values for the same routine cues. Sightings remain local visual observations. The journal and camera are tracked in [#8](https://github.com/brylie/mission-island/issues/8). Saltwind's weather and discoveries run in the standalone preview until [host integration #19](https://github.com/brylie/mission-island/issues/19). The [seasons item](https://github.com/brylie/mission-island/issues/32) is a follow-up.
