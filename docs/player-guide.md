# Playing on Starter Island

Join the same host using the [LAN instructions](../README.md#play-on-your-local-network). On your first visit, enter a game name or choose **Skip for now** to play as Crew 1 or Crew 2. The host saves your name and shows it above your character. Change it later in the crew panel or **Options → Crew appearance**.

## Controls

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| **W A S D** or **arrow keys** | Walk relative to the camera. In the truck or skiff, W/S move forward or reverse and A/D steer. |
| **E** | Use the action shown in the prompt. Pick up or deliver cargo, work a farm plot, contribute to a market order, open the job board, or work on a neighbor's repair. |
| **F** | Board or leave a stopped truck, learner vehicle or skiff. The first player takes the wheel. Both skiff seats can paddle. |
| **Shift** | Hold to sprint on foot or drive slowly and steer gently. |
| **Space** | Jump on foot or hold to brake while driving. |
| **Left click** | Reach or use an equipped tool. Hold and release to throw a crate or cast the fishing line. |
| **Right click** | Hold to raise your right hand while on foot with that hand free. Your crewmate can see the gesture. |
| **B** | Buy one carrot seed at Seabrook Market. |
| **1 / 2 / 3** | Equip or stow your fishing rod, hoe, or watering can. |
| **R** | Keyboard shortcut for spraying a held extinguisher. |
| **C** | Switch between the island overview and the close third-person view. |
| **Mouse** | Look around your character or vehicle. The first gameplay keypress captures the mouse. Aim the reticle at something you can use to choose it: it turns gold and a tooltip names the thing and the key. With nothing under the reticle, **E** uses whatever is nearest. |
| **Esc** | Release the mouse. Press a gameplay key to capture it again. |
| **J** | Open the crew's field journal. Press **J** again or **Esc** to close it. |
| **V** | Raise or lower your binoculars while on foot. They come up on whatever the reticle points at. While they are up, the mouse or **W A S D** look around (hold **Shift** to sweep faster), the readout under the reticle gives the distance and compass bearing, **left click** or **Enter** marks the spot for the crew, **X** clears your mark, and **V** or **Esc** lowers them. |
| **O** | Open Options for your name, appearance, mouse look (invert either axis, look speed), graphics detail, sound (ambience and effects levels, mute), and **Respawn at home**. |

Keys are bound by their position on the keyboard, not by their label, so on an AZERTY keyboard **W A S D** are **Z Q S D**.

Bring crates to the tail of the truck to set them on the bed. Amber orbs appear along the recommended route once a crate is loaded. Jump over the low rear lip and ride along on the bed as the truck moves. The side rails and rear lip help keep crates aboard through turns. At Seabrook, step out, collect each crate from the bed, and press **E** with the crate inside the glowing teal delivery circle. The beach has a shallow wading shelf. You slow down as the water gets deeper.

**Options → Graphics → Detail** is **Automatic** at first: the game starts at High and, if frames stay slow for a few seconds, switches to Low for the rest of the session, says so on screen and under the setting. Choosing High keeps full detail; choosing Automatic again starts back at High and checks the frame rate afresh. **Low (older computers)** draws at a lower resolution, turns off shadows and shows detailed trees and undergrowth only close by. **High** keeps the full look. Your choice is remembered in this browser.

Sound starts after your first click or key press. Surf grows as you near the shore, and your boots sound different on gravel paths, pavement, grass, leaf litter, sand, dock boards and in shallow water, duller and squelchier once rain soaks the ground. The flatbed idles while someone is in the driver's seat and strains as it pulls away or climbs. Wind is strongest on the ridge and the shore and hushes among trees, where the crowns rustle as the leaves blow. Ferns, rushes and marram rustle as you push through them, gulls call around the harbor and over the lift sites, and songbirds sing in the woods by day. Both crewmates hear a crate land, from where each of them stands, and a harder drop sounds heavier than a gentle set-down. Every cue also has visible feedback, so muting changes nothing you need to finish a job. **Options → Sound** sets ambience and effects levels or mutes this browser.

Jump onto or over low rocks, crates and fallen logs. The bed or hood gives you a second jump onto the cab roof.

Each starter crate delivered pays two coins into the shared crew bank. Completing the delivery pays three more, and clearing the lookout fire pays four. The truck's headlights and taillights come on around dusk, along with street lamps in the settlements.

The truck has four-wheel suspension and follows the terrain under each wheel. **W** accelerates, **S** brakes when moving forward and then reverses, and **Space** applies the brake without changing direction. Brake lights work in daylight and at night. Cargo and players riding on the bed move with the truck, so slowing down before sharp corners and rough ground helps keep the crates aboard.

## Learning to drive

Two learner vehicles teach the first driving for careers on Saltwind. The red Fire and Rescue pickup waits beside Fire Supplies, and the green Island Farm tractor waits north of the farm plots. Walk up and press **F** to take the wheel; a crewmate can ride in the pickup's passenger seat. The tractor seats only its operator.

While you ride, the prompt, the objective card and the map name the practice stop. A gold ring with orange cones marks it on the ground, and the map shows it as a hollow gold ring. Pull up inside the ring and brake to a full stop with **Space**; the prompt tells you when to pull forward or finish stopping. The driver earns 10 career XP, the passenger sees the result, and the vehicle's next stop comes up for the whole crew, so partners can take turns at the wheel. Each vehicle runs a **practice tour** that visits its stops once each, in order, and the stop's lesson shows as a notice once you stop in its ring. The pickup's rescue tour is a 12-stop circuit of about 1.7 km from Fire Supplies: the Fire Watch, Island Farm and Harbor Depot, then up the Highwood fire road past a culvert, the Cairn turnoff and Alder Creek, round the Pine Draw hairpin, Cairn's ridge and the summit pad, and home down the fire road. One solo tour pays 120 XP, enough to qualify. The tractor's slower tour goes to Seabrook Market, the meadow slope and back to the farm. After the last stop the tour ends with a summary of what each driver earned. To drive it again, step out and press **E** at the driver's door, or aim at the cab from anywhere beside it. [Learner driving](mechanics/learner-driving.md) has the details.

| Vehicle | Career XP | At 100 XP |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fire and Rescue pickup | Emergency driving | Qualified for the Saltwind ambulance, fire engine and patrol car once their driving jobs open |
| Island Farm tractor | Farm machinery | Qualified for a Saltwind farm tractor once its driving jobs open |
| Flatbed | Driver XP from hauled crates | Slatecliff ore truck certification |

Career XP is personal to the driver and is saved with your crew profile. The crew guide shows your progress on each path.

### Fire and Rescue gear

The pickup carries a wilderness rescue kit in its bed: two compact extinguishers and a first aid kit, with a radio, whistle and flashlight against the locker, and a ring buoy and rope throw bag on the locker lid. Aim at a piece to choose it. While the pickup is stopped, stand at the bed and press **E** to take a piece. Carry one at a time, press **E** to set it down anywhere, or press **E** at the bed to stow it again. Stowed gear rides along wherever the pickup goes, and gear carried into danger returns to the bed when you respawn.

| Gear | Left click |
| --- | --- |
| Extinguisher | Hold to spray foam. At the Forest Lookout fire it helps put the fire out, like the Fire Supplies pair. |
| First aid kit | Check on the nearest crewmate within a couple of metres. The crew sees that all is well. |
| Flashlight | No click needed: it shines a beam ahead while held, from dusk until dawn. |
| Radio | Tells the whole crew where you are. Partners see the place and how far and which way to go, such as "radioed in near Forest Lookout · 60 m north-east". |
| Whistle | Lets the crew know where you are, with the same distance and direction. |
| Ring buoy and throw bag | Hold and release to throw, like a crate. They stop short of walls, float on water, and a swimmer can grab them with **E**. |

The gear is the start of family-friendly wilderness fire and rescue missions: it has uses today, and future callouts will give each piece a job.

## Repeatable orders

After the lookout fire, press **E** at the Harbor Depot job board to take the next crew order. You can read the board from the mission panel anywhere on the island. Deliver seed crates to Island Farm, take beach gear to Driftwood Beach, or fill fish and carrot orders at Seabrook Market. Either player can contribute to an order. You can keep fishing, farming, or exploring while the order is open.

Wildfires break out now and then once the starter shift is done. When smoke is reported, climb the Fire Watch tower beside Fire Supplies and press **E** at the fire finder for a bearing, or look for the smoke column. Red fire stations at the depot, Seabrook, the farm, the tower and Pinewoods each hold an extinguisher you can take at any time. Hold left click near the flames to spray, and press **E** at any station to refill. Anyone can also raise their binoculars with **V**, aim at the flames or the ground at the foot of the smoke (the rangefinder reads ground, water and buildings, not the smoke itself), read the distance and bearing, and click to mark it: the mark shows to the whole crew as a tall beacon and a pin on the map, and a mark beside the flames tells everyone where the fire is. Larger fires spread if left alone, pay less the more they spread, and need more than one extinguisher. [Fire watch](mechanics/fire-watch.md)

Each completed order deposits its posted reward once in the crew bank. Both islands show the same balance. Either player can use it to buy seeds or a uniform. Four completed orders open the ferry terminal repair. Coins are available to spend along the way. See the [crew orders specification](mechanics/crew-orders.md) for payouts and rules.

## Restoring the ferry terminal

Harbor Depot road ends at the ferry terminal, behind a striped barrier. Once your crew has finished four harbor orders, read the tally board at the corner of the freight yard beside the barrier. Each of the three repair stages needs coins from the crew bank and timber crates from the Seabrook timber stack, on the verge south of the main road west of the beach trail. Aim at the coin box to donate, or at the order slip to have timber stacked at Seabrook. Load the timber onto the flatbed, drive back down Harbor Depot road, pull into the yard and set each crate down by the pallet. The board shows who gave what. Each finished stage changes the landing, and the last one raises the barrier so the flatbed can drive straight onto the ramp. See [the ferry terminal](mechanics/ferry-terminal.md) for the details.

## Helping Wren

Wren lives in the lilac-roofed cottage just east of the Blue cottage. The garden gate drags a groove in the path and sticks a short way open. Try it and you can't squeeze through. Talk to Wren at the cottage, or at the mending bench beside Harbor Depot road, at the edge of the ferry freight yard, during the day. Wren lends you the red toolbox on that bench. Inspect the hinge with **E**, pick up a hinge pin from the tray on the bench, and fit it at the gate with the toolbox close by. Then swing the gate to test it. One of you can do all of it, or split the work: one inspects and holds the toolbox while the other fetches the pin. The crew bank gets five coins once, and Wren remembers who helped. If your crewmate leaves with the toolbox, ask Wren to put it back on the bench. Come back the next morning to see Wren use the gate. See [neighbor repairs](mechanics/neighbor-repairs.md) for the details.

## Getting to know the neighbors

Mara fishes from the small dock, Ines keeps the Seabrook Market stall, and Bram keeps the freight tally beside the Harbor Depot job board. Each walks to work in the morning and home in the evening on the shared clock. Aim at a resident and press **E** to talk. When someone needs a hand, the conversation offers **Agree to help**. Mara would like two carrots, Ines two fish, and Bram a crate set down in the yellow bay beside the depot. Either of you can agree, and either can finish it. The crew bank gets five coins once, the resident trusts the crew a little more, and their greeting changes. Talk again another day and they'll have something new to say about the weather, the island or the neighbors.

Some jobs end with telling the resident how it went. If Wren asks whether the gate swings clear, tell the truth. Saying it does when you saw it catch costs Wren's trust, and it takes the crew a repair and two more favors to win it back. See [residents and favors](mechanics/resident-favors.md).

## Mara’s field outing

Find Mara at the small fishing dock during the day. Talk to her for walking directions to Shellglass Tide Pool and Juniper Hollow. Either player can open **Field journal → Mara’s outing** to reread the clues. You can split up and compare entries when you get back.

Press **E** when the prompt offers to inspect a detail or record an observation. At Driftwood, look for pink foxglove bells on the dune slope and a line of weed and shells farther east near Shellglass. Shellglass has a mended tide staff and small conical limpets clamped to rock. At Juniper, the trail cairn leads to low white clover with three leaflets and pale flower heads. Stand back to watch brown hares lower their heads to graze. During heavy rain, look for a hare crouching under a pine or beside a fallen trunk.

Each new journal record gives the crew one observation XP. If you have already discovered Shellglass or Juniper, reading its landmark completes an outing step using the existing place entry. Grazing and sheltering are separate observations. Talk to Mara again after the first outing record for her advice about the rain.

## Windstone Ridge gliding

Follow the northern hills toward Windstone Ridge. At the orange loan rack, face the windsock and find the landing circle below. Press **E** with free hands to borrow the wing and launch for free. Use **A/D** or **←/→** to bank and turn. Push **W** or **↑** to lower the nose and gain speed. Pull **S** or **↓** to raise the nose and slow the descent. Press **C** to switch views. Follow the airborne blue orbs and land inside the circle. If you come up short, head back uphill to the windsock.

A partner can press **E** beside the orange landing sign to turn the landing circle teal on both screens. After the checkout, either player can borrow the wing for the Mirror Pool survey. The rack gives the clue before you launch. Fly past the orange marker by the fallen willow on the east bank, then land in the circle to save the observation. Once the survey is recorded, the rack lends the wing to survey the whole island from the air. Every place you can discover on foot has an aerial survey. Pass over it with some height, then land anywhere safe to record it for the crew. To go farther, climb in rising air. Lilac motes over windward slopes lift you at any hour. Gold motes over warm fields, rooftops and rock rise on sunny days and die away in rain and at night. The HUD shows your height, the nearest lift and the next place to survey. The wing returns to the rack after every flight. Rejoining after an interruption puts the pilot on reachable ground, and the crew keeps its saved qualification and survey.

The blue survey orbs point directly to the willow, then switch to the landing circle after the observation. For an optional climb, bank left briefly after launch toward the rising lights off the ridge shoulder. Hold a bank to turn more tightly back inland with the extra height.

## Starting tools and island life

Press **E** at the chest outside your starting cottage to take your tools and one free carrot seed. While holding a crate, aim toward the truck bed and hold **left click**, then release to throw. A tap makes a short throw.

Equip the fishing rod and aim into the water from the shore, the harbor dock, or a stopped skiff. Hold **left click**, then release near the top of the rising and falling cast meter to cast farther and improve your odds. When a fish bites, hold **left click** to reel and release when it pulls. Watch line tension and land the fish before it escapes. To take the skiff, walk into the shallows beside the dock and press **F** near it. Both seats can paddle. Return to shallow water and stop before disembarking.

At Island Farm, till with the hoe, plant with **E**, then water by hand or wait for rain. Watered carrots grow in daylight. Sell fish and harvested carrots at Seabrook Market’s striped awning with **E**. Buy another seed for one coin with **B**.

## Clothes and work outfits

Press **E** at the wardrobe outside your cottage, or open **Options → Crew appearance → Open wardrobe**. Mix everyday pieces and choose face and hair options. Drag the preview to orbit or use **Front** and **Back**. Use **Apply** to show your changes to the crew or **Cancel** to keep wearing your current outfit.

Free choices include Amber and Blue shirts and caps, Slate and Sand trousers or shorts, dark and brown deck shoes, bare feet, and a bareheaded look.

At Seabrook Market, press **E** at the clothing counter beside the striped awning. Buy the Coral shirt for one crew coin, the Lagoon swimsuit for two, or the Harbor angler outfit for three. Each purchase goes into the buyer’s wardrobe and can be worn again for free. Select the swimsuit under **Shirt or swimsuit**. It covers your top and bottoms while worn, and your chosen trousers or shorts are ready when you change back.

Use **Browse** at the counter to filter hats, tops and swimwear, bottoms, footwear, or complete outfits. Each of the four everyday racks has ten pieces, priced from one to six crew coins across the collection. Your first crate earns enough for a Dockside Flat Cap or Quayside Canvas Sneakers. A completed farm supplies order pays for the Bramble Argyle Cardigan or Buttercup Wellies. You can try on a piece in the wardrobe before you own it.

The delivery and lookout fire missions unlock work outfits for both crewmates. Each player can wear one independently and return to their saved everyday clothes.

The island clock runs one day every eight real minutes. At night, press **E** at your cottage door. Dawn begins when every connected player turns in. Press **E** again or walk away to cancel while waiting.

Both of you share one field notebook, opened with **J**. It notes a fish when you land it and a place when you discover it. Aim at an animal, a plant, or a landmark and press **E** to jot a note. New subjects and behaviors earn shared observation XP. See [field journal](mechanics/field-journal.md).

Follow the northern hills to discover landmarks. Their names fade with distance in the follow view and stay on your island overview after discovery. The minimap keeps the coastline, biomes, route lines, and a simpler set of symbols. One world unit is one metre. The buoy line sits 75 metres offshore.

## Saves and reconnecting

The host automatically saves in `.mission-island/saves/island-5173.json` for the usual port. The island clock, missions, farm, item positions, crew names, discoveries, the field journal, and personal progress survive a restart. The same browser profile reclaims its crew identity. For a separate named save, start the host with a lowercase slot name.

```sh
MISSION_ISLAND_SAVE=weekend npm run dev
```

Run one host per save. Use `MISSION_ISLAND_SAVE=off` for a temporary playtest. Open tabs keep the last rendered scene during reconnect and a styled loading screen shows the current phase when the page reloads. Skiff seats are reserved for two minutes during a reconnect and move with the skiff. Use **WASD** to swim if you land in deep water, or choose **Options → Respawn at home**. Respawning brings your inventory and safely releases loose cargo.
