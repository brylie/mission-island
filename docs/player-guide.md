# Playing on Starter Island

Join the same host using the [LAN instructions](../README.md#play-on-your-local-network). On your first visit, enter a game name or choose **Skip for now** to play as Crew 1 or Crew 2. The host saves your name and shows it above your character. Change it later in the crew panel or **Options → Crew appearance**.

## Controls

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| **W A S D** or **arrow keys** | Walk relative to the camera. In the truck or skiff, W/S move forward or reverse and A/D steer. |
| **E** | Use the action shown in the prompt. Pick up or deliver cargo, work a farm plot, contribute to a market order, open the job board, or work on a neighbor's repair. |
| **F** | Board or leave a stopped truck or skiff. The first player takes the truck wheel. Both skiff seats can paddle. |
| **Shift** | Hold to sprint on foot or drive slowly and steer gently. |
| **Space** | Jump on foot or hold to brake while driving. |
| **Left click** | Reach or use an equipped tool. Hold and release to throw a crate or cast the fishing line. |
| **Right click** | Hold to raise your right hand while on foot with that hand free. Your crewmate can see the gesture. |
| **B** | Buy one carrot seed at Seabrook Market. |
| **1 / 2 / 3** | Equip or stow your fishing rod, hoe, or watering can. |
| **R** | Keyboard shortcut for spraying the extinguisher. |
| **C** | Switch between the island overview and the close third-person view. |
| **Mouse** | Look around your character or vehicle. The first gameplay keypress captures the mouse. Aim the reticle at something you can use to choose it: it turns gold and a tooltip names the thing and the key. With nothing under the reticle, **E** uses whatever is nearest. |
| **Esc** | Release the mouse. Press a gameplay key to capture it again. |
| **O** | Open Options for your name, appearance, mouse look (invert either axis, look speed), and **Respawn at home**. |

Bring crates to the tail of the truck to set them on the bed. Amber orbs appear along the recommended route once a crate is loaded. Jump over the low rear lip and ride along on the bed as the truck moves. The side rails and rear lip help keep crates aboard through turns. At Seabrook, step out, collect each crate from the bed, and press **E** with the crate inside the glowing teal delivery circle. The beach has a shallow wading shelf. You slow down as the water gets deeper.

Jump onto or over low rocks and crates. The bed or hood gives you a second jump onto the cab roof.

Each starter crate delivered pays two coins into the shared crew bank. Completing the delivery pays three more, and clearing the lookout fire pays four. The truck's headlights and taillights come on around dusk, along with street lamps in the settlements.

The truck has four-wheel suspension and follows the terrain under each wheel. **W** accelerates, **S** brakes when moving forward and then reverses, and **Space** applies the brake without changing direction. Brake lights work in daylight and at night. Cargo and players riding on the bed move with the truck, so slowing down before sharp corners and rough ground helps keep the crates aboard.

## Repeatable orders

After the lookout fire, press **E** at the Harbor Depot job board to take the next crew order. You can read the board from the mission panel anywhere on the island. Deliver seed crates to Island Farm, take beach gear to Driftwood Beach, or fill fish and carrot orders at Seabrook Market. Either player can contribute to an order. You can keep fishing, farming, or exploring while the order is open.

Each completed order deposits its posted reward once in the crew bank. Both islands show the same balance. Either player can use it to buy seeds or a uniform. Four completed orders mark the crew ready for the future second island. Coins are available to spend along the way. See the [crew orders specification](mechanics/crew-orders.md) for payouts and rules.

## Helping Wren

Wren lives in the lilac-roofed cottage just east of the Blue cottage. The garden gate drags a groove in the path and sticks a short way open. Try it and you can't squeeze through. Talk to Wren at the cottage, or at the mending bench beside the ferry approach during the day. Wren lends you the red toolbox on that bench. Inspect the hinge with **E**, pick up a hinge pin from the tray on the bench, and fit it at the gate with the toolbox close by. Then swing the gate to test it. One of you can do all of it, or split the work: one inspects and holds the toolbox while the other fetches the pin. The crew bank gets five coins once, and Wren remembers who helped. If your crewmate leaves with the toolbox, ask Wren to put it back on the bench. Come back the next morning to see Wren use the gate. See [neighbor repairs](mechanics/neighbor-repairs.md) for the details.

## Getting to know the neighbors

Mara fishes from the small dock, Ines keeps the Seabrook Market stall, and Bram keeps the freight tally beside the Harbor Depot job board. Each walks to work in the morning and home in the evening on the shared clock. Aim at a resident and press **E** to talk. When someone needs a hand, the conversation offers **Agree to help**. Mara would like two carrots, Ines two fish, and Bram a crate set down in the yellow bay beside the depot. Either of you can agree, and either can finish it. The crew bank gets five coins once, the resident trusts the crew a little more, and their greeting changes. See [residents and favors](mechanics/resident-favors.md).

## Windstone Ridge gliding

Follow the northern hills toward Windstone Ridge. At the orange loan rack, face the windsock and find the landing circle below. Press **E** with free hands to borrow the wing and launch for free. Use **A/D** or **←/→** to bank and turn. Push **W** or **↑** to lower the nose and gain speed. Pull **S** or **↓** to raise the nose and slow the descent. Press **C** to switch views. Follow the airborne blue orbs and land inside the circle. If you come up short, head back uphill to the windsock.

A partner can press **E** beside the orange landing sign to turn the landing circle teal on both screens. After the checkout, either player can borrow the wing for the Mirror Pool survey. Fly past the orange marker by the fallen willow on the east bank, then land in the circle to save the observation. The wing returns to the rack after every flight. Rejoining after an interruption puts the pilot on reachable ground, and the crew keeps its saved qualification and survey.

The blue survey orbs point directly to the willow, then switch to the landing circle after the observation. For an optional climb, bank left briefly after launch toward the rising lights off the ridge shoulder. Hold a bank to turn more tightly back inland with the extra height.

## Starting tools and island life

Press **E** at the chest outside your starting cottage to take your tools and one free carrot seed. While holding a crate, aim toward the truck bed and hold **left click**, then release to throw. A tap makes a short throw.

Equip the fishing rod and aim into the water from the shore, the harbor dock, or a stopped skiff. Hold **left click**, then release near the top of the rising and falling cast meter to cast farther and improve your odds. When a fish bites, hold **left click** to reel and release when it pulls. Watch line tension and land the fish before it escapes. To take the skiff, walk into the shallows beside the dock and press **F** near it. Both seats can paddle. Return to shallow water and stop before disembarking.

At Island Farm, till with the hoe, plant with **E**, then water by hand or wait for rain. Watered carrots grow in daylight. Sell fish and harvested carrots at Seabrook Market’s striped awning with **E**. Buy another seed for one coin with **B**.

## Clothes and work outfits

Press **E** at the wardrobe outside your cottage, or open **Options → Crew appearance → Open wardrobe**. Mix everyday pieces and choose face and hair options. Drag the preview to orbit or use **Front** and **Back**. Use **Apply** to show your changes to the crew or **Cancel** to keep wearing your current outfit.

Free choices include Amber and Blue shirts and caps, Slate and Sand trousers or shorts, dark and brown deck shoes, bare feet, and a bareheaded look.

At Seabrook Market, press **E** at the clothing counter beside the striped awning. Buy the Coral shirt for one crew coin, the Lagoon swimsuit for two, or the Harbor angler outfit for three. Each purchase goes into the buyer’s wardrobe and can be worn again for free. Select the swimsuit under **Shirt or swimsuit**. It covers your top and bottoms while worn, and your chosen trousers or shorts are ready when you change back.

Use **Browse** at the counter to filter hats, tops and swimwear, bottoms, footwear, or complete outfits. Each of the four everyday racks has ten pieces, priced from one to six crew coins across the collection. Your first crate earns enough for a Dockside Flat Cap or Quayside Canvas Sneakers. A completed farm supplies order pays for the Bramble Argyle Cardigan or Buttercup Wellies. You can try on a piece in the wardrobe before you own it.

The delivery and lookout fire missions unlock work outfits for both crewmates. Each player can wear one independently and return to their saved everyday clothes.

The island clock runs one day every eight real minutes. At night, press **E** at your cottage door. Dawn begins when every connected player turns in. Press **E** again or walk away to cancel while waiting.

Follow the northern hills to discover landmarks. Their names fade with distance in the follow view and stay on your island overview after discovery. The minimap keeps the coastline, biomes, route lines, and a simpler set of symbols. One world unit is one metre. The buoy line sits 75 metres offshore.

## Saves and reconnecting

The host automatically saves in `.mission-island/saves/island-5173.json` for the usual port. The island clock, missions, farm, item positions, crew names, discoveries, and personal progress survive a restart. The same browser profile reclaims its crew identity. For a separate named save, start the host with a lowercase slot name.

```sh
MISSION_ISLAND_SAVE=weekend npm run dev
```

Run one host per save. Use `MISSION_ISLAND_SAVE=off` for a temporary playtest. Open tabs keep the last rendered scene during reconnect and a styled loading screen shows the current phase when the page reloads. Skiff seats are reserved for two minutes during a reconnect and move with the skiff. Use **WASD** to swim if you land in deep water, or choose **Options → Respawn at home**. Respawning brings your inventory and safely releases loose cargo.
