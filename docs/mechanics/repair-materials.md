# Materials checkout and ferry path edging

Once the crew tests Wren’s mended gate, the Crew guide points to a marked fallen branch beside Meadow Slope Path, behind the cottages. Press **E** at the marker to gather a piece of timber, then turn it in at the sample tray next to Wren’s mending bench. On the next outing, follow Beach Trail and the shore walk toward Shellglass Tide Pool. The loose stone is on the dry side of Splitstone Strand. Return it to the same tray to earn the shared Materials qualification. Either crewmate can gather or return either sample. Habitat logs and embedded rocks remain in place.

At **Ferry Approach Edging**, beside the bench, approach the timber or stone display and press **E** to choose two units of that material. A pocket holds two units of each material, and a new pickup is available four seconds after the previous one. The guide and map point to the next pickup or the repair site according to what the player is carrying. The repair site shows the quantity remaining. Press **E** there to install one section from your own pocket. Each section appears on both screens as it is delivered. The second section earns the crew 5 coins and 1 Wren Trust once. Marked supplies can still be gathered after the repair.

The host saves both checkout steps, the chosen material, installed sections, source refill timers and personal pocket counts. A returning crewmate can pick up where they left off. The crewmate who stays can gather the rest and finish on their own. Both players keep access to the qualification and see the same edging after a host restart.

Use the `materials-checkout` live checkpoint for a run immediately after the gate. Try both choices across separate runs. In co-op, have each player bring a sample on a different outing, split the collection, reconnect after the first section and press **E** on both screens for the last one. Revisit the edging and record which landmarks helped the players find the sources and recognize the repair. [Starter Island playtest #52](https://github.com/brylie/mission-island/issues/52)
