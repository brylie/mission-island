# Crew orders

Finishing the starter delivery and lookout fire opens the job board at Harbor Depot. Read it from the mission panel, or approach the board and press **E** to take an order. The host accepts the first valid request when two players choose at once. One order is active at a time. The crew can fish, farm, sell other goods, or explore while the job is in progress.

| Order | Work | Pay into crew bank |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Farm supplies | Take two green-tagged seed crates from Harbor Depot to Island Farm. | 6 coins |
| Beach gear | Take three blue-tagged beach gear crates from Harbor Depot to Driftwood Beach. | 10 coins |
| Fresh fish order | Bring two fish to the Seabrook Market awning. | 6 coins |
| Carrot order | Bring two carrots to the Seabrook Market awning. | 8 coins |

Crates are staged at the depot. Put them in the flatbed, drive to the teal circle, and press **E** with each crate inside the circle. At the market, **E** contributes only the goods and amount still needed. Either player can contribute, and the payout arrives when the whole order is filled.

The host saves the active order, its progress, the count of completed orders, and one bank balance for the crew. Completing an order deposits its posted pay once, regardless of who contributed or who is online. Returning and new players see the current balance. When loading a save with personal wallets, the host pools all unspent coins, including those of offline profiles. Later restarts load the saved bank directly.

After four completed board orders the crew is marked ready for the second island. The readiness count belongs to the island save. Buying seeds or clothing draws on the shared bank and gives the purchase to the buyer. The travel route and loading behavior will be defined with the second island.

## Playtest

- Take an order while the other player is paddling or working the farm. Check that their activity and position continue.
- Complete a delivery solo, then repeat as a pair. Have one player stage and load crates while the other positions the truck. Check that both runs earn the same pay and that the roles save handling time.
- Load and deliver a farm crate. Try turning it in at the old Seabrook drop-off, then at the marked farm circle.
- Split an order across both players. Refresh one client after a partial turn-in and check that the progress and remaining quantity match.
- Disconnect one player, wait for the crew reservation to expire, and finish the job. Rejoin and check that both players see the same bank balance and that reconnecting adds no coins. Use the market to buy a seed and confirm that the second-island progress stays the same.
