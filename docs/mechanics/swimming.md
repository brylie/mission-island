# Swimming and the Driftwood shallows

Players can wade and surface-swim in everyday clothes, tread water when they stop, and swim back to shore after falling from the skiff. Keep that recovery available to both crew members, including someone reconnecting in deep water.

## Review the surface stroke

Aim for a relaxed breaststroke that someone watching from the dock can read. During the pull, the hands sweep out and gather beneath the chin as the heels draw up. During the kick, the hands reach forward and the feet sweep back together. Leave a short glide with the legs trailing. The breath should clear the waterline. On stopping, ease into an upright tread with small hand movements and the face clear of the water. [Surface stroke refinement](https://github.com/brylie/mission-island/issues/107)

Scrub one cycle in the Crew character Workbench in everyday clothes and the starter swimsuit. Look at the shoulders and straps from the front and back through the reach. Then have one player watch from the side at Driftwood Beach while their partner wades in, swims across, turns, stops and comes ashore. Swap roles. Can the observer point out the kick, the breath and the glide at normal speed? Record any sudden change in pose separately from a pulse in the water around the swimmer. [Waterline effects](https://github.com/brylie/mission-island/issues/106) · [Shared playtest record](https://github.com/brylie/mission-island/issues/52)

## Planned outing with a mask and snorkel

At Driftwood the crew can already watch a live school from shore. The planned kit gives one player a reason to follow it into a sheltered shallow cove. The crew bank pays for the mask and snorkel, and the buyer owns and equips them. Both pieces together unlock a face-down surface view and short breath-hold dives. Reserve looking down for aiming the camera. Give diving its own deliberate input and make the way back to the surface visible throughout. [Snorkel gear and ownership](https://github.com/brylie/mission-island/issues/74) · [Driftwood school](../driftwood-fish-school.md)

Try a daylight route past a gap in the kelp to shells among the stones. A solo swimmer should be able to follow the sloping bottom out and back. In co-op, have one player describe the school from the shore or skiff while the equipped partner follows a flank below. Swap roles once both have gear and ask what each vantage point revealed. Test surfacing beside the hull and reconnecting mid-dive after the skiff has moved. The returning swimmer should regain control at the surface and be able to swim home.
