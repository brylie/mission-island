# Charged throws

Press **E** to pick up a loose crate. Aim with the follow camera and use **left mouse button down** to charge the throw. Release to launch. A tap gives a short throw, and power rises for 1.05 seconds before holding at full. In island view, use the crew character's facing direction. Start behind the truck and aim along the middle of the bed to practice.

The host timestamps the press and release and gives the crate an initial velocity. Launch speed ranges from 2.2 to 8.6 meters per second forward and 3.4 to 4.5 meters per second up. The crate can bounce off a rail, settle and ride on the bed, or land nearby. A crate that falls into water beyond wading depth returns to its starting point. Use **E** to set the crate down, place it on the bed, or deliver it inside the marked drop zone. The starter delivery goes to Seabrook. Follow the active order's marker for repeat deliveries.

The action binds to the crate held when the button went down. Switching items, opening Options, releasing pointer lock, or losing focus cancels a windup. The extinguisher continues to use left-click for spray.

## Playtest

- Tap and hold the button from the same spot on open ground. Confirm that a longer hold travels farther.
- Toss a crate over the flatbed's low rear lip. The crate should settle aboard and ride with the truck. A crate passing high above the bed should still be airborne.
- Charge, open Options, and release the mouse. The crate should stay in your hands. Check the same sequence with pointer lock on and off.
