# Long Acre pollinators and honey trial

Open **Landscape Workbench → Scenic view → Long Acre Fields pollinators** to inspect the field margin on Saltwind. The dry, rain, and recovery links use the same camera and a paused frame 6.4 seconds into the study. Run Vite on its default port to open them.

| Conditions | What to watch |
| --- | --- |
| Dry morning | Bees pause on clover, trefoil, or phacelia and fly between the three beds. The stem gives a little when a bee settles. Choose another island day to change the order of visits. |
| Sustained rain | Bees take cover at the hives. They rest near the entrances and activity stops around the flower beds. |
| After rain | Scrub from zero to see the staggered return. Bees visit the sheltered clover by the hedge before resuming their wider circuit. |

In live weather the field samples time and recent rain to produce the same result for a given clock. The Saltwind preview runs its own clock in each browser. The Workbench links restore an identical time and weather history for comparison.

At the matched frame the readout showed four feeding and two flying in dry weather, none flying or feeding in sustained rain, and five feeding and one flying during the return to clover. The links reopened in Brave with the same camera and frame.

## One jar to Barleycross

In the Saltwind preview, choose **Site map → Long Acre Apiary → Inspect site**. Use **E** or the action button at each stop.

1. Borrow the veil and take one clean jar from the gate shelf (185.7, 26.4).
2. Walk around the east end of the rail to the covered extractor (184.1, 22.0). The capped comb frame, hand crank, and tap show where the honey comes from. Fill and cap the jar there.
3. Carry the jar by hand to the honey shelf at the **Barleycross Farmers Market** (160.6, −39.5). The trial pays **4 coins** on hand-in.

Four coins is a tuning value. One jar makes for a short local errand, with a free loaned veil and no vehicle purchase. Try bundling several jars into a crate after the single delivery feels good. A vehicle becomes useful when an order bundles multiple crates or goes to a more distant market. A full suit is a possible requirement for a future hive-opening job.

The browser playtest walked from the kit shelf to the extractor and on to the market, handed in the jar, and checked that a second interaction could not pay again. The visible shared crew bank balance was the same before and after the trial.

The preview records one delivery for the current session. The four-coin trial payout is shown in the order panel. Host-authoritative rewards, saves, and multiplayer travel can follow when Saltwind is integrated with the shared crew simulation.
