# Starter Island economy and pacing sheet

Use one row per crew session to answer two questions. When does the crew first recognize it has earned spendable money? How much work could a visible stage of the ferry repair reasonably ask of a crew that also wants seed or clothing?

One crew bank spans both islands. Count each payment once even when both people see it. Four completed board orders open the ferry repair. Its stage costs are trial values, and travel is planned. Recheck amounts against the [order rules](../mechanics/crew-orders.md), [fishing](../mechanics/fishing.md) and [farm and market rules](../mechanics/farming-and-market.md) when tuning changes.

## Compare two routes to the same contribution

**Trial assumption.** Use 20 coins as a *paper budget for one repair stage*. The figure is a question for playtesting. It sets neither a ferry price nor an unlock condition. Begin both examples just after the opening missions with 13 coins and all starter seeds unspent.

| Example route | Crew ledger | What to time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Haul for the depot | 13 + 6 for farm supplies = 19. A second haul paying 10 brings it to 29, leaving 9 after the hypothetical contribution. | Time to choose, load and finish each available order. Note time with nobody handling cargo. |
| Fish and grow food | Buy a seed for 1, raise two carrots using that seed and a free starter seed, and catch one fish. Selling the harvest and catch brings 13 − 1 + 6 + 2 to 20, leaving 0. | Time until the first sale and until both crops are ready. Note whether fishing gives the crew something it wants to do while crops grow. |

Use the orders actually offered in a timed run and record substitutions. Two fish sold directly earn 4. Those same two fish fulfilling an order earn 6 in total. Ask whether spending the last coin felt worth it, and whether the crew would rather contribute supplies directly. Try the same comparison with one player and with two people sharing roles.
