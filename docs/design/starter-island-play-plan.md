# A working week on Starter Island

**Proposed playtest plan.** The crew should have a satisfying local routine before the ferry opens and enough reasons to keep living here afterward. Use the [Seabrook service map](seabrook-village-services.md#service-map-and-scouting-points) to place the daily visits, the [habitat plan](https://github.com/brylie/mission-island/blob/6b77e7cc2aadb3f12ed41e7f6ffacfa49ca58cc2/docs/design/starter-island-biomes.md) to choose outings, and the [story and cadence brief](https://github.com/brylie/mission-island/blob/6b77e7cc2aadb3f12ed41e7f6ffacfa49ca58cc2/docs/design/starter-island-story-and-cadence.md) for the multi-week arc.

The first habitat circuit follows ordinary work from Seabrook through Island Farm to Driftwood and Shellglass. Use it to see whether crews can read the changes in scenery and sound while getting somewhere they already have a reason to visit. [Biome implementation issues #176](https://github.com/brylie/mission-island/issues/176)

## One sample week

Days show a possible sequence a crew takes. They are playtest prompts. Keep unfinished work visible when the crew sleeps, and let skipped offers surface again through their ordinary source. The crew guide can highlight one useful next step while the players pursue their own plans. Use the weekly market, seasons and event schedule as tuning questions. [Activity scheduler #148](https://github.com/brylie/mission-island/issues/148)

| Day | Reason to head out | What might stick |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1 · Find your way home** | Meet at the cottages, check the chests and run the first Harbor Depot delivery. Take turns driving and loading. Ask each player where the first money went. | One shared payout, the road back to the cottages and the closed ferry ramp. [First-payout playtest](../playtests/starter-island-first-payout.md) |
| **2 · Be useful nearby** | Try Wren's gate or another small favor. One reads the problem while the other operates the fixture. If the crew wants paid work, finish the opening shift or try a farm or fish sale. | The repaired gate swings clear. Return after sleep to see whether Wren uses the shortcut and remembers either crewmate. |
| **3 · Read the shore** | Ask Mara for an outing, compare two fishing spots or watch the Driftwood school from shore and water. Either player can make the journal record. | The partners can compare what they saw. Come up to Shellglass for a different shoreline condition or take the beach path back through the farm. |
| **4 · Market morning** | One sells produce while the other collects Tove's parcel. Meet under cover, compare the address to the houses and choose how to carry it. | One parcel payout, a recognizable front door and a meeting spot worth using again. [Market morning route](seabrook-village-services.md#first-two-player-playtest--market-morning) |
| **5 · A quiet working day** | Harvest, mend something already promised, change clothes or take a short walk. Try rain or dusk so doors, lamps and residents' routines matter. | Players can spend a day on work they chose. Record any prompt that makes the day feel interrupted. |
| **6 · Try a new responsibility** | Choose one local checkout. Try hauling, the farm barrow, Materials, ridge flight, or another career as its entry becomes playable. Take the previously untried role if playing as a pair. | A new tool, permission or job is immediately useful. Each crewmate can explain how to use it. |
| **7 · Make a plan for next week** | Revisit a favor, gear up for an outing or ask Bram how the wharf can move forward. Stop at home or the square before calling it a day. | Ask what each person wants to do next and whether they know where to start. |

In **week two**, try one new responsibility in a setting that changes the approach, such as a different delivery bay or a second site to compare in the field journal. In **weeks three and four**, make the wharf a shared project with visible stages. When materials and funding start a full day of construction, offer an ordinary job, a quiet outing or time at home. Continue to let the crew play on both sides of the reopening. A market visit, a return load, or a seasonal change to a familiar path can make the first morning after the opening feel worth staying for. Use the current readiness and cost rules as the comparison and record active minutes alongside island days. [Construction stages #169](https://github.com/brylie/mission-island/issues/169) · [Economy and pacing sheet](starter-economy.md)

## Starter career and reward map

Start each major skill area in a familiar place and let either player use the crew's qualification. Keep the practical work feasible solo, with useful roles for a partner and a reason to swap. The next job tests whether the skill transferred. It can change the load, terrain, cue or weather. [Learning through island work](learning-and-careers.md) · [Shared careers #18](https://github.com/brylie/mission-island/issues/18)

| Path and local mentor | First skill and how two can help | Reward and next responsibility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Hauling · Bram at Harbor Depot** | Place a load and plan where to unload. Drive and spot, or deliver a parcel on foot together by reading the address and carrying it. | Starter deliveries already pay the crew. Add the planned loading checkout to open a more demanding local job. Use a new bay or awkward load before larger freight. [#50](https://github.com/brylie/mission-island/issues/50) · [#170](https://github.com/brylie/mission-island/issues/170) |
| **Growing and provisions · Ines at Seabrook Market** | Read soil and crop readiness and fill a produce order. One tends plots while the other checks quantities and moves crates or the barrow. | The garden order already earns crew pay and unlocks the farm barrow and workwear. Reuse the barrow on a larger delivery. [#95](https://github.com/brylie/mission-island/issues/95) |
| **Fishing · Mara at the small dock** | Read the bite and reeling cues. Watch Mara, then take turns making a cast or pointing out what the line is doing. | Catch sales already pay the crew. Try the missing-rod favor and a demonstration. Compare different fishing places for a later order. [#67](https://github.com/brylie/mission-island/issues/67) |
| **Small craft · Mara, with Bram for harbor work** | Steer a skiff and identify a buoy from safe water. Swap helm and spotter. | Skiff travel is playable. A planned deckhand checkout earns a local buoy inspection before later work with a motor launch or tow. [#96](https://github.com/brylie/mission-island/issues/96) |
| **Repair and materials · Wren at the bench** | Inspect, fit and test a repair. One moves the part while the other watches for the catch. Choose useful windfall or loose stone and get it to the right place. | Gate repair, shared Trust and the Materials checkout give access to known work and a visible result. Try a drain or lamp fixture with a different symptom. Try tree or rock processing later at a managed worksite if it makes the job more interesting. [#92](https://github.com/brylie/mission-island/issues/92) · [#98](https://github.com/brylie/mission-island/issues/98) |
| **Neighborhood response · Harbor board, with Nessa proposed for supplies** | Find a small fire or another local call and bring the right equipment. One locates and reports while the other handles the equipment. | The opening lookout fire already pays the crew. A second call can open a usable response kit before more complicated incidents. Use the shared scheduler to leave room after a call. [#97](https://github.com/brylie/mission-island/issues/97) · [#148](https://github.com/brylie/mission-island/issues/148) |
| **Aviation · Windstone Ridge loaner and signs** | Read the wind and landing field, fly the approach, then swap pilot and ground spotter. | Shared aviation checkout already opens a survey over Mirror Pool. Improve soaring and local flight readability before asking pilots to go farther. [#94](https://github.com/brylie/mission-island/issues/94) · [#145](https://github.com/brylie/mission-island/issues/145) |
| **Field observation · Mara, with Tove proposed for local records** | Write down what happened and under what conditions. One watches from shore while the other approaches the school, then repeat with swapped roles. | Shared journal records already award observation XP. A later resident can use repeated sightings to place a buoy or protect a habitat edge. This is an entry into research work. [#8](https://github.com/brylie/mission-island/issues/8) · [#51](https://github.com/brylie/mission-island/issues/51) |

Pay jobs and market sales into the shared bank once. Keep shared qualifications and Trust separate from personal clothing and style. Set the first local reward in terms the crew can use right away. Compare a practical purchase, such as seed, with a personal purchase, such as clothing, before making either a requirement for another activity. Ferry readiness should recognize useful work from more than one path. Treat career specialization as elective. [Crew progression and ownership](progression.md#what-belongs-to-whom)

## What to measure first

- **Market morning.** Can players find their own first stop, agree where to meet and use a house detail to identify the recipient? Count coaching prompts, hand-in attempts and idle wait time. Ask whether either person would linger.
- **A quiet day and two career preferences.** Run one hauling-led crew and one farm-and-fishing-led crew using the same milestone record. Note what each role actually did, which jobs players choose to repeat, and whether both have a useful next step while a wharf stage is underway.
- **A reason to stay.** On the morning after the wharf opens, ask where each player would spend their next island day, which resident or place they would visit, and why. Record the places and jobs they name without prompting. [Shared milestone checklist #52](https://github.com/brylie/mission-island/issues/52)

## Working names

Keep **Mission Island** in project material while trying spoken names. Seabrook can remain the village and Saltwind remains the second island. Try the shortlist aloud in a greeting and on a ferry sign.

| Name | Why try it |
| --- | --- |
| **Fernward** · first island | Takes its cue from Fernfall and the wooded uplands above the coast. “Welcome to Fernward. Seabrook is up the road.” |
| **Juniper Isle** · first island | Immediately connects the island to Juniper Hollow. Test whether players can keep the hollow and island distinct in directions. |
| **Homeward Crew** · game | Puts the team and the idea of home in the title. It can hold several islands and types of work. |
| **The Harbor We Build** · game | Expresses the opening shared achievement and the tone of working for a place. Ask whether it sounds broad enough for work inland. |
| **Island Workmates** · game | Clear and easy to say. Compare it with the others for personality and sense of place. |

Try **Homeward Crew** and **Fernward** first. “Fernward ↔ Saltwind” is the candidate ferry sign. These are working names for discussion before any project rename.
