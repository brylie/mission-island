# The crew’s progression

A session should offer a reason to work and a reason to take the long way home. Longer-term progress should open activities, equipment, relationships, and places the crew can recognize. The Starter Island milestone asks whether players feel attached to the island by the time they can leave it. [At home before the ferry](https://github.com/brylie/mission-island/milestone/4)

Each career can teach a practical skill through useful work. Start by noticing a cue and trying the technique, then open a responsibility that asks the crew to apply it in a different situation. [Learning through island work](learning-and-careers.md)

## From the cottages to ferry service

| Moment | Playable foundation | Planned next step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Arrive | Each player starts near their own cottage and tool chest. The crew can sleep through the night together. | Meet a resident, find the depot, and learn a route home that can be recalled after a late join. [First day](https://github.com/brylie/mission-island/issues/49) |
| Earn a living | Finish the delivery and lookout shift to open the harbor board. Sell a catch or harvest at Seabrook Market. | See where the pay went, try a hauling checkout, and earn access to another local responsibility. [First career qualification](https://github.com/brylie/mission-island/issues/50) |
| Become familiar | Walk to named discoveries, watch Juniper’s hares seek cover, and return in different weather. | Follow a resident’s clue, record an observation together, and hear someone recognize the discovery back in town. [Field outing](https://github.com/brylie/mission-island/issues/51) |
| Improve the harbor | Four completed board orders open the ferry terminal repair at the tally board by the barrier. Three stages each take crew coins and timber hauled from Seabrook, and the terminal changes after each one. The last stage opens the ramp to the flatbed. Saltwind can be inspected separately in a browser preview. | Tune the stage costs against both earning routes, add a harbor steward, then the first crossing and return delivery. [Ferry terminal](../mechanics/ferry-terminal.md) · [Balance](https://github.com/brylie/mission-island/issues/52) · [Crossing](https://github.com/brylie/mission-island/issues/20) |

The [Starter Island story and cadence](starter-island-story-and-cadence.md) proposes a few island weeks of local work, relationships and visible repairs before the wharf opens. The fresh-save playtest will compare that pace with the current four-order rule.

## What belongs to whom

| Record | Owner and status | What advances it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Coins | One shared bank per host save across Starter Island and Saltwind. | Each sale or job credits the crew once. Either player can spend the balance. [Orders](../mechanics/crew-orders.md) · [Cross-island wallets](https://github.com/brylie/mission-island/issues/16) |
| Tools, goods, and clothing | Personal, playable. | Bought seeds go into the buyer’s inventory. Buying the Harbor angler uniform unlocks it for that player. |
| Order and readiness | Shared, playable. | Either player can contribute to the active order. Its completion advances the island’s count once. [Orders and reconnects](../mechanics/crew-orders.md) |
| Career experience and qualifications | Shared. Aviation and Materials qualifications are playable. | Landing the Windstone Ridge checkout earns the crew's aviation qualification once, and it opens the Mirror Pool survey to either player. It carries forward to powered flight on Saltwind. Checking in windfall timber and surface stone opens Wren’s ferry path edging request to either player. The Starter Island hauling checkout is planned. [Ridge gliding](../mechanics/ridge-gliding.md) · [Powered flight](https://github.com/brylie/mission-island/issues/14) · [Repair materials](../mechanics/repair-materials.md) · [Career progression](https://github.com/brylie/mission-island/issues/18) |
| Trust | Shared per resident. Favors for Mara, Wren, Ines and Bram are playable, with stranger, familiar and trusted tiers. Wren’s swollen gate can break a promise; amends and rebuilding are playable. | Each kept favor raises that resident’s Trust once and changes their greeting. A knowing false report costs two, amends earn nothing, and two later favors restore the resident’s reliance, shown when Wren lends the toolbox freely. The broader design lets kept promises gradually change what a resident entrusts to the crew. A clear breach can cost several favors’ worth of confidence. Making amends and following through on later favors rebuild it. [Shared crew Trust](residents-and-destinations.md#shared-crew-trust) · [Neighbor repairs](../mechanics/neighbor-repairs.md) · [Residents and favors](../mechanics/resident-favors.md) · [Villagers](https://github.com/brylie/mission-island/issues/3) |
| Discoveries and observation | Personal map discoveries, shared aerial surveys, and one shared [field journal](../mechanics/field-journal.md) for fish, places, nature and behaviors. Each unique record earns one observation XP. Mara’s outing tracks eight confirmations using the same entries. | Observation progress can support rewards alongside careers. The journal can cover more species and Saltwind. |
| Ferry repair | Shared, playable. | Either player donates crew coins and stacks hauled timber. The save keeps each stage's contributions per crewmate, and the tally board names them. Finishing a stage changes the terminal once for both players. [Ferry terminal](../mechanics/ferry-terminal.md) · [Repair playtest](https://github.com/brylie/mission-island/issues/52) |

An outing for two might pair a truck run with a morning on the water. When they meet at the market, the crew can see how both activities contributed to the bank. In the planned repair loop they can then decide together what to donate and what to keep for seed. Test this with a crew that favors fishing and farming as well as one that favors deliveries. The repair budget and pace are still open.

After service resumes, Starter Island remains home. The planned Saltwind outing begins with one tagged crate and a return trip. Lodging or a second home may eventually let the crew stay away longer.
