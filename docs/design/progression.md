# The crew’s progression

A session should offer a reason to work and a reason to take the long way home. Longer-term progress should open activities, equipment, relationships, and places the crew can recognize. The Starter Island milestone asks whether players feel attached to the island by the time they can leave it. [At home before the ferry](https://github.com/brylie/mission-island/milestone/4)

Each career can teach a practical skill through useful work. Start by noticing a cue and trying the technique, then open a responsibility that asks the crew to apply it in a different situation. [Learning through island work](learning-and-careers.md)

## From the cottages to ferry service

| Moment | Playable foundation | Planned next step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Arrive | Each player starts near their own cottage and tool chest. The crew can sleep through the night together. | Meet a resident, find the depot, and learn a route home that can be recalled after a late join. [First day](https://github.com/brylie/mission-island/issues/49) |
| Earn a living | Finish the delivery and lookout shift to open the harbor board. Sell a catch or harvest at Seabrook Market. | See where the pay went, try a hauling checkout, and earn access to another local responsibility. [First career qualification](https://github.com/brylie/mission-island/issues/50) |
| Become familiar | Walk to named discoveries, watch Juniper’s hares seek cover, and return in different weather. | Follow a resident’s clue, record an observation together, and hear someone recognize the discovery back in town. [Field outing](https://github.com/brylie/mission-island/issues/51) |
| Improve the harbor | Four completed board orders mark the crew ready for a future second island. Saltwind can be inspected separately in a browser preview. | Introduce the repair, show each stage at the dedicated terminal, and restore ferry service. The first crossing and return delivery follow. [Restoration](https://github.com/brylie/mission-island/issues/4) · [Crossing](https://github.com/brylie/mission-island/issues/20) |

## What belongs to whom

| Record | Owner and status | What advances it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Coins | One shared bank per host save across Starter Island and Saltwind. | Each sale or job credits the crew once. Either player can spend the balance. [Orders](../mechanics/crew-orders.md) · [Cross-island wallets](https://github.com/brylie/mission-island/issues/16) |
| Tools, goods, and clothing | Personal, playable. | Bought seeds go into the buyer’s inventory. Buying the Harbor angler uniform unlocks it for that player. |
| Order and readiness | Shared, playable. | Either player can contribute to the active order. Its completion advances the island’s count once. [Orders and reconnects](../mechanics/crew-orders.md) |
| Career experience and qualifications | Shared. The aviation qualification is playable; the others are planned. | Landing the Windstone Ridge checkout earns the crew's aviation qualification once, and it opens the Mirror Pool survey to either player. It carries forward to powered flight on Saltwind. Elsewhere, relevant work leads to a practice job and an equipment or responsibility unlock, with hauling as the next Starter Island checkout. [Ridge gliding](../mechanics/ridge-gliding.md) · [Powered flight](https://github.com/brylie/mission-island/issues/14) · [Career progression](https://github.com/brylie/mission-island/issues/18) |
| Trust | Shared per resident. Wren’s repair award is playable. Other residents, breaches, and recovery are planned. | Mending Wren’s gate raises Trust once and changes the next morning’s greeting and route. The broader design lets kept promises gradually change what a resident entrusts to the crew. A clear breach can cost several favors’ worth of confidence. Making amends and following through on later favors rebuild it. [Shared crew Trust](residents-and-destinations.md#shared-crew-trust) · [Neighbor repairs](../mechanics/neighbor-repairs.md) · [Villagers](https://github.com/brylie/mission-island/issues/3) |
| Discoveries and observation | Named-place discoveries are recorded per player today. Aerial surveys of the same places are shared by the crew and playable. A shared field journal and observation experience are planned. | Either player will be able to record a species, place, or distinctive behavior. First records should earn credit once. [Field journal](https://github.com/brylie/mission-island/issues/8) |
| Ferry repair | Shared project, planned. | Crew coins and local supplies can become a lasting contribution. Both players should see what was added, what is still needed, and the change at the terminal. [Repair playtest](https://github.com/brylie/mission-island/issues/52) |

An outing for two might pair a truck run with a morning on the water. When they meet at the market, the crew can see how both activities contributed to the bank. In the planned repair loop they can then decide together what to donate and what to keep for seed. Test this with a crew that favors fishing and farming as well as one that favors deliveries. The repair budget and pace are still open.

After service resumes, Starter Island remains home. The planned Saltwind outing begins with one tagged crate and a return trip. Lodging or a second home may eventually let the crew stay away longer. [Saltwind first playable outing](https://github.com/brylie/mission-island/milestone/1) · [Lodging](https://github.com/brylie/mission-island/issues/17)
