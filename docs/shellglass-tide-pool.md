# Shellglass Tide Pool

Take the Driftwood Beach spur to the dry rim near (43.2, −31.4). The mended staff stands on the landward ledge at (44.65, −31.45). Press **E** to read the current phase and record the place in the crew field journal. Watch the tide mark climb and the wash cover the ledges. As the water withdraws it leaves a damp band and exposes three pockets holding water around the limpets. The wrack sits at the usual high-water line. At night, look into the pockets for plankton.

Run Vite on its default port, then open one of these views or choose it under **Landscape Workbench → Scenic view**. The first three have the same camera and daylight.

- [Retreating tide](http://localhost:5173/workbench.html?view=shellglass-retreating#landscape)

- [Low water at 23:00](http://localhost:5173/workbench.html?view=shellglass-night#landscape)

The cosmetic tide draws on the host island clock and takes 2.55 island days to repeat. The local surface rises about 21 cm. The journal uses the host observation time to show the recorded phase. The ocean and wading rules still use the existing water level.

The host playtest harness reconnects a seat while the tide is retreating and compares the phase and surface height derived from both clients' clocks. The walking test uses the starter movement simulation from Driftwood Beach through the dry rim to the Splitstone cleft, checking for water and scenery obstructions on the way.
